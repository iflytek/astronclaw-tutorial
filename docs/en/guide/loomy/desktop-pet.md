# Desktop Pet

Desktop Pet is a little sprite that lives on your desktop within Loomy. It’s more than just an animated image—it senses your AI session status, lets you unlock random personalities, reacts when you poke it (sometimes shyly), and gives live commentary as you work.

When activated, the Desktop Pet always stays on your desktop, keeping you company: it cheers you up as you start a task, waves with a comment when you finish, offers comfort if you run into trouble, and occasionally tosses out a cheeky but caring remark during downtime.

## How to Enable Desktop Pet

Go to **Settings** → **Pet**, and toggle on "Show Desktop Pet".

![Enable Desktop Pet](/loomy/desktop-pet/settings-off.png)

If you don’t have a pet yet, after enabling the feature you can click "Summon Desktop Pet" to draw one.

## Drawing and Deploying

When drawing a pet, Loomy first summons a candidate. You can choose "Draw Again" for another candidate, or click "Deploy" to set it as your current desktop pet.

![Summon Result](/loomy/desktop-pet/summon-result.png)

Each pet comes with different looks and personality styles—gentle, energetic, teasing, shy, intellectual, or dramatic, for example. The style affects the way it talks, interacts, and comments on your progress.

You don’t need to choose a personality in advance. Loomy will summon a candidate at random. Pets also come in various rarities; the rarer the pet, the more special the summoning effect and the bigger the surprise.

> **Note**
>
> Drawn pets are just candidates. The new pet only takes effect after you click "Deploy". Switching pets will clear the old pet’s memory. Please confirm before changing.

## Manage Your Current Pet

After deployment, you can view the name, personality, rarity, and a brief description of your current pet on the Pet Settings page. You can also redraw at any time.

![Pet Settings](/loomy/desktop-pet/settings-on.png)

The "Pet Chat" area below lets you adjust talkativeness, when the pet speaks, and whether to let it know you better.

## Adjust Personality & Activity

Under "Talkativeness", choose your pet’s activity level:

- **Quiet**: Only speaks at important events, rarely interrupts.
- **Medium**: Keeps you company, says something at key times.
- **Lively**: Chats more, speaks during idle times and interactions.

If you like a quiet workspace, choose "Quiet"; if you want your pet to feel like a chatty companion, pick "Lively".

## Set When the Pet Speaks

Your pet comments with short, personalized lines at these times:

- **Completion comment**: Appears after a dialog or task ends, usually summarizing your progress.
- **New task start**: Appears when starting a new task, motivating you to get into the flow.
- **Idle comment**: Speaks up if you’re inactive for a while.
- **Error comfort**: Offers comfort when you hit an error, using its own personality.
- **Daily greeting**: Shows up the first time you use Loomy each day.
- **Long break return**: Appears after being inactive more than 4 hours.

The pet tries not to interrupt when Loomy is busy. During active tasks, checks, or when waiting for your response, it stays quiet to avoid interfering.

## Let Your Pet Know You

Enable "Let Pet Know You" so the pet can combine recent interactions and Loomy's recent memory to give more personalized comments.

Your pet remembers past interactions and forms impressions. It doesn’t just say random lines—it learns your pace, the types of tasks you do, and when you’re likely to get stuck.

> **Privacy Note**
>
> Pet comments will not appear in your formal chat list.

## Ways to Interact with It

The pet supports various desktop interactions:

- **Single click**: Tap it to trigger a reaction bubble.
- **Repeated taps**: Tap it several times for different reactions; tap too many times and it may "play dead" for a while.
- **Double click**: Bring Loomy main window to the foreground; sometimes triggers a hidden line.
- **Drag**: The pet runs toward your pointer as you drag.
- **Drag to screen corner**: Triggers a "cornered" comment.
- **Scale**: Sometimes complains about being squished.
- **Long hover**: Stare at it for a while and it gets shy.

![Pet on Desktop](/loomy/desktop-pet/pet-idle.png)

Different personalities express themselves differently, and the same action can trigger various responses.

![Pet Comment Bubble](/loomy/desktop-pet/pet-comment.png)

## Right-click Menu

Right-click on the pet for a quick menu:

- **Open Loomy**: Return to the main Loomy program.
- **Open AI Search Box**: Quickly launch the AI search bar.
- **Exit Pet**: Close the current desktop pet.

![Pet Context Menu](/loomy/desktop-pet/context-menu.png)

## When Should You Use the Pet?

Desktop Pet is for those who want a stronger sense of companionship while working. It doesn’t replace your AI Companion or change Loomy’s core task abilities. Instead, it’s an emotional and feedback layer on your desktop—AI collaboration isn’t just in your chatbox anymore.

Think of it as a buddy who observes your progress: quietly accompanying you, emerging for comments at key milestones, sometimes getting flustered if you poke it, and always ready to celebrate your completed tasks with you.
